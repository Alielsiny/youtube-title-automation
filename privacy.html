Privacy Policy

This application ("YouTube Title Automation") is a personal automation tool used solely by its owner to update the title of their own YouTube video based on view count, using the YouTube Data API.

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data belonging to any third party. It only accesses the YouTube channel data of its own developer/owner through OAuth, strictly to read view counts and update video titles on that same account.

No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising purposes.
